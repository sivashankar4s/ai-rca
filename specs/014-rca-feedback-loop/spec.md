## Summary

Let operators mark an RCA as correct or incorrect, and feed that signal back to improve
root-cause signature matching and calibrate the confidence score over time.

## In scope (when specified)

- Correct/incorrect feedback capture on a case.
- Feedback-driven adjustment of signature matching and confidence calibration.
- Persistence of feedback for trend analysis.

## Dependencies

- Builds on existing signature matching (`signature_service`) and the confidence score
  introduced by `007-agentic-investigation`.
